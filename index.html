The place

You are not buying a plot. You are buying Malnad.

Thirthahalli sits where the Western Ghats begin to hold the monsoon. Areca and paddy, the Tunga running through, Agumbe up the ghat. Land here is bought by people who intend to come back to it — for a second house, for retirement, for children who will want it later.
